How to Use FingerCanvas

  1. 1
    Open Your Camera
    Click the Open Webcam button. Allow camera access when your browser asks.
  2. 2
    Pick Your Colour
    Choose a preset colour swatch or click the colour wheel to pick any custom colour.
  3. 3
    Set Your Brush Size
    Drag the slider to choose a brush from 2 px (fine line) to 80 px (thick stroke). The live circle shows the exact size.
  4. 4
    Paint!
    Click and drag with your mouse — or use your finger on a touchscreen — directly on the live video to draw.
  5. 5
    Eraser / Undo / Clear
    Eraser — switch to eraser mode.
    Undo — remove your last stroke.
    Clear — wipe the whole canvas (animated!).
  6. 6
    Download Your Art
    Click Download Art to save a PNG snapshot of your video + drawing. Enjoy the confetti! 🎉
  7. 7
    Keyboard Shortcuts
    C Clear canvas   E Toggle eraser   D Download art

Tip: Enable Mirror for a natural selfie-style view.
